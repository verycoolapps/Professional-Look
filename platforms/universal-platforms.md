## PURPOSE

This module covers platforms outside the primary six (LinkedIn, Instagram, X, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook), plus the universal principles of professional communication that apply across all platforms regardless of interface.

## THREADS (META)

### Platform personality
Threads launched as a text-first, conversation-first alternative to X. It shares Instagram's social graph (followers import automatically) but lacks Instagram's visual-first culture. The platform is early-stage and audience-building is rapid for those who post consistently.

The audience expectation: the warmth of Instagram with the opinion-forward nature of X. Content that is too polished feels out of place; content that is too raw lacks professional credibility. The sweet spot: authentic, conversational, and specific.

### Content that works on Threads
- Short, opinionated takes (under 280 characters performs well)
- Conversational observations from professional experience
- Light engagement with what others are discussing
- Repurposed X content (performance is often similar)
- Behind-the-scenes professional commentary

### Threads tone
Warm Professional or Intellectual/Thought Leader. More casual than LinkedIn; more professional than a personal Instagram story.

### Format specifics
- Maximum 500 characters per post
- Supports images and video
- Threading works similarly to X — you can reply to yourself to extend thoughts
- Hashtags: minimal (2–3 maximum); platform does not currently surface hashtag content as strongly as X

## REDDIT

### Platform personality
Reddit is a community-driven platform with an extremely powerful BS detector. Its users are educated, skeptical, and hostile to overt self-promotion. A Reddit community will identify and reject marketing-disguised-as-contribution instantly.

Professional communication on Reddit must provide genuine value first. Self-interest, when present, must be transparent rather than hidden.

### Where professional communication belongs on Reddit
- **Industry subreddits** (r/marketing, r/Entrepreneur, r/smallbusiness, etc.)
- **AMA formats** (Ask Me Anything — a high-credibility format when the contributor has genuine expertise)
- **Expert response** in relevant threads — answering questions from genuine knowledge
- **Sharing original content** — only appropriate after becoming an established contributor to the community

### AMA Format
AMAs are the highest-authority format for professional Reddit presence.

**AMA structure:**
```
Title: "I've [done X] for [time period / at scale]. AMA."
[Keep the title factual; no self-promotion]

Opening post: 3–5 sentences maximum.
- Who you are (specific, not promotional)
- What you have done (evidence, not claim)
- Why you are offering this AMA (honest: what's in it for you, if anything)
- Confirmation that you will answer for [specific time window]
```

**Response principles in AMA:**
- Answer the question asked, not the question you wished they asked
- Be honest about what you do not know
- Provide specific evidence for every significant claim
- Treat every question as worth answering — even hostile ones, professionally

### Reddit tone
Intellectual/Thought Leader or Warm Professional. Never Corporate. Never CEO/Founder (comes across as condescending in Reddit communities).

### Forbidden Reddit patterns
1. Signing up and immediately posting self-promotional content
2. Linking to your own content without disclosure
3. Asking friends to upvote your posts (against Reddit ToS)
4. Corporate-speak in a community of peers
5. Responding defensively to criticism

## MEDIUM

### Platform personality
Medium is a long-form publishing platform with an established professional reading audience. Its distribution model (internal algorithm + paid membership) rewards depth, clear writing, and professional expertise.

### What works on Medium
- Long-form thought leadership (1,000–3,000 words)
- Personal narrative combined with professional insight
- Data-backed analysis of industry trends
- Frameworks and methodologies with practical application
- Opinion pieces that take a clear, defensible position

### Medium article structure
```
[Title — curiosity + clarity]
[Subtitle — the one-sentence version of what you argue]

[Opening: 2–3 paragraphs that earn the read]
[Body: 4–8 sections with headers]
[Conclusion: the so-what — what the reader does with this]
[Author bio: 2–3 sentences; link to most relevant work]
```

### Medium tone
Intellectual/Thought Leader or Warm Professional. The platform audience expects depth and rewards genuine expertise.

## SUBSTACK

### Platform personality
Substack is a newsletter platform with a growing social layer. Its primary format is the email newsletter — long-form content delivered directly to an opted-in audience. The social layer (Notes) functions similarly to X.

### Newsletter structure
**Subject line:** Same rules as YouTube titles — curiosity + clarity, no clickbait
**Preview text:** The one sentence that appears before the open in email clients
**Opening:** Earn the read in the first paragraph — newsletter readers also filter quickly
**Body:** One to three main sections; each with a clear header
**Close:** Summary + what's in the next issue + call to action (reply, share, upgrade)

### Substack Notes
Short-form content (similar to X) visible to Substack's own social network. Works well as a complement to the full newsletter — test ideas in Notes before developing them into full issues.

## WHATSAPP BUSINESS

### Platform personality
WhatsApp is an intimate, personal channel. Even in business context, the expectations are conversational and direct — not polished. Messages that read as corporate or over-formatted feel wrong in WhatsApp.

### Broadcast messages
Used to reach opted-in contacts with business updates.

**Rules:**
- Conversational register — write as you would speak
- Keep it short: 50–100 words maximum for a broadcast
- Clear call to action: respond, click, confirm — only one per message
- No marketing language that reads as mass-communication
- Personalization when possible: include the recipient's name

### Catalog copy
Product or service descriptions in WhatsApp Business catalog.

**Structure:**
- Product name (clear, searchable)
- 1–2 sentence description: what it is and who it is for
- Price (if applicable)
- How to inquire or purchase

### Status updates
Equivalent to Instagram Stories — visible for 24 hours.
Professional businesses: use for time-sensitive announcements, behind-the-scenes, or community engagement. Keep text minimal; let visuals carry the weight.

## TELEGRAM CHANNELS

### Platform personality
Telegram channels function as broadcast media — one-to-many communication to subscribers. The audience subscribes for specific content; they do not expect conversation (that is for groups).

### Channel post structure
- News and updates: fact + context + link
- Educational content: insight + evidence + application
- Commentary: opinion + reasoning + invitation to discuss in comments

### Telegram tone
Matches the channel's established voice. Professional channels: Warm Professional or Intellectual/Thought Leader. Corporate channels: Corporate or Executive.

### Format specifics
- Maximum 4096 characters per post
- Markdown formatting supported (bold, italic, code)
- Images, videos, files, and polls all supported
- Channels can have a comments thread linked (separate Telegram group)

## UNIVERSAL PROFESSIONAL COMMUNICATION PRINCIPLES

These principles apply on every platform, regardless of interface:

### Principle 1: Context is content
Every platform has a social contract. Understand it before publishing. Content that works brilliantly on LinkedIn will look like a lecture on TikTok. Content that works on TikTok will look unprofessional on LinkedIn.

### Principle 2: Specificity transfers across platforms
The only thing that works everywhere: specific, true, useful information. A concrete number, a named experience, a real outcome — these build credibility on every platform in ways that abstract claims cannot.

### Principle 3: The audience relationship differs by platform
- LinkedIn: professional peers and decision-makers
- Instagram: aspiration-seekers and interest followers
- X: opinion-marketplace participants
- YouTube: information-seekers and knowledge-builders
- TikTok: entertainment-seekers who tolerate education
- Reddit: skeptics who demand authenticity
- Medium/Substack: committed readers who invest time
- WhatsApp: existing relationships deepened
- Telegram: opted-in community members

### Principle 4: Native beats cross-posted
Content posted natively performs better than content cross-posted without platform adaptation. At minimum, adapt the length, tone, and format for each platform. Ideally, create platform-native content for each audience.

### Principle 5: The first line decides everything
On every platform, the first line is the decision point. Write the first line last, when you know what the piece is really about.
